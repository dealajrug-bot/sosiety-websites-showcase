## What you are viewing

These are **work-in-progress website previews**, captured on **September 19, 2026**. This GitHub showcase was assembled on **October 11, 2026**. The screenshots document that version of the work; they are not live hosted websites, and buttons or forms pictured inside them are not interactive here.

The three experiences remain distinct: fiction and reader discovery, business requirement preparation, and experimental technology research. Publication details, business delivery arrangements, connected contact services, and security-product claims require their own supporting evidence.
